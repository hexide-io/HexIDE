Why the remaining A rows stay unchanged:

ObjectBrowserViewIntegrationTests: the view render checks measure/arrange the browser
UserControl directly; all other rows assert properties, commands, history or class/member
collections on a Dock Document model. None hosts a DocumentControl, tab strip or DockControl.
CanClose/CanFloat assert model policy, not realised tab chrome. Inventing a dock host here
would change their subject rather than strengthen the existing assertion.

InspectMembersTests: DescribeDataContext(new DockedVm()) reflects a Dock-derived model;
it checks omitted base members, not docking or a template.

CarriedFileDiagnosticsIntegrationTests: URI filtering and cleared markers assert VM state;
save tests assert server calls and file-write ordering. Their Show helper instantiates an
editor, but the claim rests on the model or protocol, not the rendered text surface.

MinimapControlTests: the two LineMapping tests check line/row arithmetic against a
TextDocument, with an editor only to supply the document. The click-to-scroll test also
calls the editor scroll path and is B; it still does not claim real pointer dispatch.

AddinToolWindowReHostTests: the test constructs two AddinToolWindowViews directly over
a Tool-derived VM in a StackPanel. Its claim is cached-content reparenting, observed via
GetVisualParent(), not a Dock move through DeferredContentControl. No Dock template is
instantiated, so no theme-dependent assertion is available without replacing the scenario.
Classified A under the no-such-assertion rule; it is not coverage of Dock drag/rehosting.
