### Added

- **ListBox and ComboBox now expose `NewIndex` and per-item `ItemData`** (#5106). Values follow sorted and explicit insertions, removals, text replacements and appends, including checkbox lists, with VB6's measured direct-call types, conversions and errors.
- **Saved ListBox and ComboBox items load from their `.frx` records** (#5114), together with their decimal Long data and no initial selection. Saved order and the original companion bytes are retained; undecodable records refuse before showing a partial list. The form designer still draws a saved ListBox as an empty box (#5121).
