### Changed
- **An add-in signed under a root this HexIDE doesn't carry now says so** (#5316). It used to look exactly like an
  unsigned or broken one. Packages now carry the public key of their root, and when the whole chain verifies under that
  key, Options → Add-Ins names its fingerprint and asks you to compare it with a newer release's TRUST.md. The add-in is
  still refused: HexIDE never trusts a root key a package brings. `pack` writes the key from the trust folder's
  `hexide-root.pub` and refuses one that didn't sign the intermediate. HexIDE builds from before this change refuse
  packages that carry the key.
