### Fixed

- **Forms and PictureBoxes raise Paint and support Refresh** (#991). Paint follows showing, growth,
  restoring and visibility changes, after Activate on first Show. Refresh raises Paint before returning
  and restores drawing kept by AutoRedraw; drawing made with AutoRedraw False is discarded by Refresh.
  AutoRedraw True suppresses Paint, and PictureBoxes raise Resize when their size changes while shown.
