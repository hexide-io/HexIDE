### Added

- **Slider's object model** (#1091 A): MSComctlLib's range, value, steps, ticks, selection, orientation and tip properties now work from code, with measured validation and Change events.

- **Slider input and drawing** (#1091 B): keyboard steps, channel paging, thumb dragging and wheel input follow the
  measured Scroll/Change ordering. Ticks, selection, orientation, disabled thumb and focus are drawn.

- **Slider file loading and round trips** (#1091 C): persisted properties load in the measured order, including file-only inverted ranges, clamping and ignored Text. VB6's saved Slider form round-trips byte for byte, preserving explicit SelStart. Slider own-property editing remains view-only.

- **Preserving Slider property lines** (#1091): Slider own property lines are carried unchanged, including file-only ranges and selection lines. Own-property editing remains view-only.

### Fixed

- **Slider forms remain read-only** (#1091) until designer property edits can be saved faithfully, with either LF or CRLF line endings.
