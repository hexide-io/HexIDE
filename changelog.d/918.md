### Fixed

- **VB6 control edges draw their 3D and Flat bands** (#918). CheckBox and OptionButton wells use the sunken system-colour pair, or two WindowFrame pixels at Flat. TextBox models and saves Appearance, drawing a sunken pair or one WindowFrame pixel; BorderStyle 0 keeps its previous drawing. CommandButton's raised edge uses whole pixels, retaining its pressed sunken style and caption placement.
