### Fixed

- **View and code-window context menus use free access keys where available** (#3121). Latin-script
  packs keep the first entry's key when labels clash and move later entries to their first free letter,
  preferring word starts. Already unique keys stay unchanged; sharing remains where no letter is free.
  VB6's own mnemonics were not measured: this follows HexIDE's menu-order policy rather than claiming
  VB6 mnemonic fidelity.
