### Added

- **A component class can say where its control goes when its tool is double-clicked** (#5681). `IDesignerAddedPlacement` gives the bounds a double-clicked control takes, and whether Align to Grid moves it. The Basic provider's StatusBar, FlatScrollBar and ImageList answer through it, so the form designer no longer names those classes, and each still lands where VB6 puts it. A class that throws, or gives bounds the designer can't place and save, is logged, and its control is centred as any other.
