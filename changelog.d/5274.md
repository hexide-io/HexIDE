### Fixed

- **Saving a `VERSION 4.00` form preserves its palette mode** (#5274). A `VB.Form` without
  `PaletteMode` now gains `PaletteMode = 1 'UseZOrder` when saved as `VERSION 5.00`, matching
  [VB6's measured upgrade](../docs/vb6-fidelity-oracle/serialization--file-format-fidelity.md).
  Existing palette lines and the verbatim designer text shown before saving are preserved.
