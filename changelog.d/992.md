### Fixed

- **SavePicture writes the measured picture formats instead of stopping the run** (part of #992).
  It preserves BMP pixels and indexed palettes, saves the measured GIF subset as an indexed BMP,
  JPEG as a 24-bit BMP, and a single 32×32 icon as an icon, regardless of the destination extension.
  Empty pictures and missing folders raise the measured VB6 errors; unmeasured layouts still refuse.
