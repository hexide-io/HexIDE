### Added

- **The form designer takes VB6's scale rules from the provider that registered them** (#5758). `IHexIdeHost.RegisterDesignPositions` registers what gives a control's Left, Top, Width and Height in the units the Properties window shows them in, a typed position's pixels, and the units a Line's endpoints are held in. Only the Basic provider may register it, one at a time, because its answers decide the positions written into a form. The Properties window, the toolbar's readout, the form designer and automation ask it, so none of them names the provider's types any more, and positions show and convert as before. A throw, or an answer the IDE can't use, is logged. A position is then shown as the model holds it, a value typed in is refused with nothing changed, and a Line isn't moved.
