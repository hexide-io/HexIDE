### Changed

- **Control libraries register through `IHexIdeHost.RegisterControlLibrary`** (#5599). The Basic provider registers its eight, and a library is asked what controls it offers only when something first needs to know. HexIDE's grant table now names the type-name namespaces each owner keeps: `VB` and the provider's libraries' names. A class in one of those namespaces is refused to any other owner, whether or not the provider has registered yet, and a library registers only under a namespace its owner's grant keeps.
