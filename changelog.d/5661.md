### Security

- **Disabling an add-in, revoking its consent, or a revocation list naming it, takes out everything it registered at
  once** (#5661). Its
  menus, commands, tool windows, Options pages and project templates now go immediately, as its language servers
  already did, including ones it registered after it started. Before, a revoked add-in's later tool windows and
  Options pages stayed until HexIDE restarted. Its code still unloads at the next restart, and Options says so.
