### Fixed

- **`press_key` refuses a disabled control** (#2366). A disabled control can't take focus, so the tool raised the key
  on it as a routed event, which no person's keyboard could do, and reported `"success": true`. It now refuses, as
  `interact`'s `click` and `invoke` do, saying the control is disabled and nothing was pressed. `inspect_element`
  reports `isKeyboardFocusable` false for such a control, where it read true.
