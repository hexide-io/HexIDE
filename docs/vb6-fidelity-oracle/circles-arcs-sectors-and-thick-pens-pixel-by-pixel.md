## Circles, arcs, sectors and thick pens, pixel by pixel (2026-11-18, #988, SP6)

**The question.** [The drawing methods page](the-drawing-methods-line-pset-circle-cls-and-point.md) measured a circle's
extent and a few thick-pen shapes. Which pixels does `Circle` light, outline and all, for any radius, aspect, arc,
sector and fill? And what do thick pens draw beyond the shapes measured there?

**The probe:**
- **The form:** 120 by 120 pixels, `ScaleMode` 3 and `AutoRedraw` True, run in session 0. p-code and native code
  logged the same grids. `circle r10` and `circle r20` were re-run on the guest's console (2026-11-18, #4768), the
  IDE, p-code and native agreeing, and matched the same run's session 0 pixel for pixel, and r10 the grid below.
  That is what to expect if `Point` reads the persistent bitmap, which session 0 has too (reasoned, not checked).
- **Each case:** it set `DrawWidth = 1`, `FillStyle = 1` (transparent) and `FillColor = 0`, then ran `Cls`. It drew
  once, in the default ForeColor unless a colour is named. Then it read every pixel of x and y 14 to 66 with `Point`.
- **The grids:** `#` is any pixel that isn't the background read at (2, 2) after the `Cls`; `R` is red and `G` is
  green. Each row starts at x 14, and only rows with something drawn are shown. Each case also logged CurrentX and
  CurrentY.

### What the grids show

- **A circle at DrawWidth 1 is the ellipse whose box runs from the centre minus the radius to the centre plus the
  radius, inclusive, on both axes.** At (40, 40):
  - r10 lit x and y 30 to 50;
  - r20 lit 20 to 60;
  - r3 lit 37 to 43;
  - r1 lit four pixels round the centre, and not the centre;
  - r0 lit nothing.
- **A fractional radius or centre moves the box's edges.** r10.5 at (40, 40) lit 29 to 51. Centre (40.5, 40.5) is kept
  as 608 twips, CurrentX 40.53333, and r10 there lit 31 to 51.
- **The radius is the longer half-axis.** r12 with aspect 0.5 lit x 28 to 52 and y 34 to 46; with aspect 2, y 28 to 52
  and x 34 to 46.
- **Aspect 0 and -1 draw.** Aspect 0 lit one row, x 30 to 50. Aspect -1 lit the same pixels as the plain r10 circle.
- **An arc runs counter-clockwise from its start angle to its end, 0 being to the right.** Start 0, end π/2 lit the
  upper right quarter. Start π/2, end 0 lit the other three quarters.
- **A negative angle draws a radius from the centre to that end of the arc**, which makes a sector. Its arc is the arc
  the angles' magnitudes name.
- **A filled circle's edge is the plain circle's outline.** With FillStyle 0 and FillColor green, `Circle (40, 40),
  10, vbRed` drew red on exactly the pixels the plain r10 circle lit, and green on every pixel inside.
- **The ends of arcs and radii, and thick pens, follow no rule defended here.** The grids are the record:
  - A circle at DrawWidth 2 and 3 isn't symmetric.
  - At width 2 a horizontal line is two rows, one pixel shorter at its start on the upper row.
  - A zero-length `Line` at width 3 lit a five-pixel plus. `PSet` at width 3 lit eight pixels, on the drawing methods
    page.
- **CurrentX and CurrentY end at the centre** for every `Circle` here: arc, sector and filled alike. For `Line` they end
  at its second point, and for `PSet` at its point.

## PSet at DrawWidth 9, 11 and 12 (2026-11-19, #4841, SP6)

**The question.** The grids above measure `PSet` at widths 2 to 8 and 10, but not 9. #4813 draws only measured widths,
so it leaves width 9 refused in the middle of the range. Does width 9's dot follow from 8's and 10's?
And what do 11 and 12 draw?

**The probe:** as above.
- **The form:** 120 by 120 pixels, `ScaleMode` 3 and `AutoRedraw` True, run in session 0. p-code and native code logged
  the same grids.
- **Each case:** `DrawWidth = 1`, `FillStyle = 1` and `Cls`, one `PSet` in the default ForeColor, then every pixel of x and
  y 14 to 66 read with `Point`. `#` is any pixel that isn't the background.
- **A control:** width 10 was drawn again first. Its grid is the same, row for row, as the **pset w10** grid above.

### What the grids show

- **Width 9 is 9 pixels wide and 10 tall.** At (40, 40) it lit x 36 to 44 and y 36 to 45: 70 pixels.
- **Width 11 is 11 by 12,** x 35 to 45 and y 35 to 46. **Width 12 is 12 by 12,** x 34 to 45 and y 35 to 46.
- **The boxes follow one pattern over every width measured, 2 to 12:** an even width's dot is a square of that size, and
  an odd width's is one pixel taller than it is wide. Which pixels inside the box are lit follows no rule defended
  here; the grids are the record.
- **A fractional point moves the whole dot.** Width 9 at (40.5, 40.5), held as 608 twips (CurrentX 40.53333), lit the
  same shape one pixel right and one down: x 37 to 45, y 37 to 46.
- **CurrentX and CurrentY end at the point,** as at every other width.
