# A real mouse resting over the IDE takes a staying hover back, and no tool can move it

**Symptom (2026-10-28, Windows, the workstation's unattended desktop, #2562's live check).** The real cursor rested at
(697, 565) on screen, over the IDE window. Three times out of three, `hover(target:
"…/Button[Standard.SaveProject]", window: "ide", stay: true)` replied that the button `does not read as under it
(isPointerOver is false): the real mouse moved the pointer to (696.7, 542) in the window … during the watch`, and the
tip was gone. Windows sends a mouse move at the cursor's resting point whenever a window appears or changes under it,
and the tip's own popup is such a window. Avalonia then moves pointer-over to what is under the real cursor.

**Workaround.** Park the real cursor off the IDE's windows before the hover, outside the tools: with the cursor on the
taskbar, the same call held and `inspect_element` gave `isPointerOver: true`. A caller limited to the hexide tools has
none.

**Suggested fix.** #2666 lists three options, none decided:
- while a hover stays, ignore a foreign move at the point of the last one;
- a tool that parks the real cursor;
- leave it, since the reply names the cause.
