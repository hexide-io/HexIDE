# On native Wayland, `hover`'s first tooltip of a session reported `tipBounds` at 0,-30, read before the compositor placed it

**Symptom.** Measured 2026-11-18 with `--platform wayland` under GNOME on openSUSE Leap 16, at #3603's build, Avalonia 12.1.6.
The IDE was maximised at 1024x768, and its window coordinates sat at (+2, +59) on the console. The session's first tooltip:

```
hover {"target":"Window/Custom[MainView]/None[#0]/None[StandardToolbar]/Button[Standard.SaveProject]","window":"ide"}
→ "tip: Save Project (its top-left 107 left of and 65 above the point hovered); seen open about 441ms after the move"
  "tipBounds":[0,-30,77,21]
```

The next seven calls matched the console within 2 px: Save Project `[107,25,77,21]` and Open Project `[85,25,79,21]`,
alternating with the tip closed in between. So 1 of 8 reads was wrong, and the reply gave no sign of it.

**Cause, reasoned and not traced.** (0, -30) is a popup at 0,0 less the 30 px from the window's surface top to its client
area. So the popup's position hadn't come from the compositor, in `xdg_popup.configure`, when `hover` read it on first seeing
the tip open (`UiAutomationDriver.OpenTipBounds`).

**Workaround.** Hover the same control again: the second read comes after the popup is placed. A `tipBounds` at or above
the window's top edge, far from the point hovered, is the tell.

**Suggested fix.** On native Wayland, read a popup's place only once the compositor has configured it: wait, bounded, for
its first position change. Or leave `tipBounds` out with a note while it hasn't arrived. Check whether `inspect_element` and
`take_snapshot` straight after a popup opens can hit the same race; a menu read at once after `interact expand` was right.
Filed as [#4627](https://github.com/hexide-io/HexIDE/issues/4627).
