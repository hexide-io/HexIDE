# Make's replace-the-archive question was invisible to its reply — **CLOSED** (#2718, 2026-10-28)

**Symptom.** At the seam between #2690 and #2695, read in batch 108's combined `ProjectService.MakeProjectInternal`.
- **The question came after the Writing stage:** a second Make to the same path asked "Replace these existing files?"
  for the archive beside the program.
- **So the reply waited it out:** `MakeReply.AwaitAsync` waited its 60 s limit, then said Make was "still writing the
  program".
- **No was misreported:** it left through the cancelled-picker arm, and was reported as "the save dialog was cancelled".
- **The batch hung on it:** batch 108's own `MakeOutcomesTests` hung here, with the box unanswered.

**Workaround.** Dump the window named "HexIDE" while the reply waits, and answer the box with `interact`. A caller then
learned nothing of what Make did.

**Fixed.**
- **A stage for the question:** `MakeOutcomes.Stage.Asking`, with the archive's path.
- **An immediate reply:** while the question is open, `MakeReply` replies after a second at most, naming the archive and
  saying that `interact` answers it.
- **Its own reason for a No:** `replacing … was declined, so nothing was written`, with no box open.
- **interact reports the ending:** an `interact` that answers the question appends what Make then did, as
  `invoke_menu_item`'s reply does.
- **Tests:** `MakeOutcomesTests`.
- **Live check:** a headless first-contact run made three times to one path, ending at a decline and then a replace.
