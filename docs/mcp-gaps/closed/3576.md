# On native Wayland, `get_window_state` reports 0,0 and `set_window_state` can't move a window, and neither says why — **CLOSED** (#3576, 2026-11-10)

**Symptom.** Measured 2026-11-10 with `--platform wayland` (#3568) under GNOME 48 on openSUSE Leap 16, against a
capture of the VM's console taken from outside the guest.

```
get_window_state {"window":"auto"}        (the About dialog)
→ {"state":"Normal","x":0,"y":0,"width":607,"height":311,"client":{"x":9,"y":39,"width":607,"height":311}, ...}
```

On the console that dialog's top left was at (658, 267).

```
set_window_state {"state":"Normal","x":100,"y":80,"width":1200,"height":800}
→ {"success":false,"error":"The window manager has not confirmed the requested change.",
   "window":{"state":"Normal","x":0,"y":0,"width":1200,"height":800,"client":{"x":9,"y":39,...}, ...},
   "note":"The window manager did not confirm the requested state or geometry within 1 s of that request; ..."}
```

The state and size were applied, and the compositor placed the window at (650, 325). Wayland gives a client no way to
read or set the position of its own top-level window, so a retry or a longer wait won't change this. A 0,0 that
reads as a measurement and a refusal that reads as a timing problem both send a first-time caller the wrong way. With
no flag, through XWayland, `get_window_state` read `"x":0,"y":32` for a window the console showed at that place.

**Workaround.** Pass only `state`, `width` and `height` under native Wayland, and don't read `x`, `y` or the `outer`,
`frame` and `client` origins as screen positions. Filed as
[#3576](https://github.com/hexide-io/HexIDE/issues/3576).

**Suggested fix.** Where the platform has no window position, leave out `x`, `y` and the screen origins, or say in the
reply that they aren't known. Refuse `x` and `y` in `set_window_state` up front with that reason, and still apply
`state`, `width` and `height` when those were passed alone.

**Fix.** On native Wayland `get_window_state` leaves out `x`, `y` and `client`, and its `note` says why and that `width`, `height`
and `state` are still read. `set_window_state` refuses `x` and `y` before anything changes, with that reason, and `state`, `width`
and `height` still work alone. Verified live on 2026-11-10 by hexide-mcp, under `--platform wayland` on GNOME 48:
`set_window_state {"state":"Normal","x":100,"y":80,"width":1200,"height":800}` replies `success:false` with "On native Wayland only
the compositor can place a window, so 'ide' can't be moved, however long you wait. ... Nothing was changed.", at once and with no
1 s wait. The same call without `x` and `y` replies `success:true` with 1200 × 800. With no flag, through XWayland, the call with
`x` and `y` still moves the window to 100, 80.
