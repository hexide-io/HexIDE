# Inspecting a language-server card's text repeats long member values — **CLOSED** (#3027, 2026-11-04)

**Symptom (Windows, 2026-11-04, #3025 live check).**
`inspect_element(target: "Window/Custom[MainView]/Pane[DockControl]/Pane[Root dock]/List[PART_ItemsControl]/List[#0]/Pane[#0]/Custom/ScrollViewer/List[#0]/List/Text[Id]", window: "ide")`
returned 12,230 characters and 30 members. OutputText occupied 4,164 characters, cut at 4,000 of 39,987;
CapabilitiesJson occupied 4,785. Fourteen other card texts returned 12,201–12,263 characters each.
Twenty layout reads cost about 245 KB. A chain step's `Text[Connecting]` returned 1,188 characters.
These measurements are recorded in [#3027](https://github.com/hexide-io/HexIDE/issues/3027).

**Workaround.** Inspect the control that shows the value, such as its text box or the Immediate window's
editor, to read up to 4,000 characters as `value`.

**Fix.** Member values are cut at 200 characters; a control's own `value` keeps the 4,000 cap, now also
applied to value providers after dropdown selection resolution. Suffixes state full lengths. The note
counts cut members and explains the workaround, preserving existing notes. The schema is unchanged.
Headless tests cover a DataTemplate with 30 members, two long, plus TextBox and TextEditor values.
On WSL (2026-11-04), the templated fixture's serialized reply fell from 11,350 to 3,904 characters;
four regression tests failed with the original driver and all 11 member-inspection tests passed with the fix.
Live check passed (hexide-mcp, 2026-11-04, Windows): a card's `Text[Id]` gave 4,078 characters, with
`CapabilitiesJson` cut at 200 of 548 and the note. A running form's 9,000-character TextBox gave
`[truncated at 4000 of 9000 chars]`, where that branch had no cap before, and the Immediate window gave
4,000 of 17,492 as its value and 200 for its Document member. A fresh session read the new description sentence.
