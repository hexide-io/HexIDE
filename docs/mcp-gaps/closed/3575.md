# On native Wayland, `interact` `click` presses away from its target, and `take_snapshot` and `boundingRect` are offset by the title bar — **CLOSED** (#3575, 2026-11-10)

**Symptom.** Measured 2026-11-10 with `--platform wayland` (#3568) under GNOME 48 on openSUSE Leap 16, against a
capture of the VM's console taken from outside the guest. The title bar and shadow are drawn inside the window's own
surface, and the tools disagree about whether a point includes them. The inset is what `get_window_state` reports as
`client`: (0, 30) maximised and (9, 39) otherwise.

```
interact {"target":"Window/Custom/Button[OK]","action":"click"}        (the About dialog)
→ {"success":true,"mechanism":"pointer","detail":"clicked the left button at (37.5, 12.5) on 'Button',
   which is (303.5, 286.5) in the window, landing on its part 'AccessText'; ..."}
```

The dialog stayed open, and the "Add-in signing keys" expander 39 pixels above OK opened instead.

```
interact {"target":"Window/Custom[MainView]/None[#0]/Menu[MenuBar]/MenuItem[File]","action":"click","window":"ide"}
→ {"success":true,"mechanism":"pointer","detail":"... which is (17.5, 10.5) in the window, landing on its part 'AccessText'; ..."}
```

`expandCollapseState` stayed `Collapsed`, because the press went to the title bar, 30 pixels above File. With no flag,
through XWayland, the same call opens the menu.

`inspect_element {"target":"Window/Custom/Button[OK]"}` gave `boundingRect` `[275,313,75,25]` for a window whose
client height is 311. On the console, OK is at about (267, 274) in the client area. File reads `[1,32,33,17]` with the
flag and `[1,2,33,17]` without it.

`take_snapshot {"window":"ide","inline":true}` returned 1920x1018 with a blank band 30 pixels deep at the top and the
status bar cut off the bottom. `take_snapshot {"inline":true}` on the About dialog returned 607x311 with its content
39 pixels down and OK outside the image. With the File menu open, the first call drew the popup at the top left of
the image, over the blank band, though the console shows it correctly placed under File.

The reply to `click` is what misleads. The hit test runs on the translated point and names the intended target, but
the press goes elsewhere. Reasoned, not checked: raw pointer input is in the surface's coordinates and `TranslatePoint`
to the `TopLevel` isn't, so a point from one read as the other is off by the inset. `hover` raises input the same way
and wasn't measured.

**Workaround.** `interact`'s `invoke`, `expand` and `collapse`, `press_key`, `type_text` and `invoke_menu_item` all
worked correctly with the flag, so use `invoke` where a control has the provider. Nothing substitutes for `click` on
a control with no provider. For true pixels, capture the compositor rather than the window. Filed as
[#3575](https://github.com/hexide-io/HexIDE/issues/3575).

**Suggested fix.** Convert between the `TopLevel`'s coordinates and the platform's input coordinates wherever raw
input is raised, render the snapshot from the content origin, and give `boundingRect` and the window's client origin
one coordinate space. The reply shouldn't claim a landing the press didn't reach. A headless test can't show this,
because it needs a window with client-side decorations.

**Fix.** Since Avalonia 12.1 a `Window` is the child of an internal host, which is what input is delivered to, and client-side
decorations set it in from that host. The reasoned cause above was right. `interact` and `hover` now convert from the window's
coordinates to the host's in one place, for the raw input they send and the pointer events they raise on a control, and convert
back when they read input. `boundingRect` is measured from the window. `take_snapshot` draws the host and keeps the window's
rectangle. The sentence above that a headless test can't show this was wrong: `DecoratedWindowTests` puts Avalonia's own inset on
a headless window by reflection.

Verified live on 2026-11-10 by hexide-mcp, under `--platform wayland` on GNOME 48, against captures of the console:
`MenuItem[File]` reads `[1,2,33,17]` as it does without the flag, and `click` opens it. In a restored window, `Button[OK]` reads
`[264,274,75,25]`, which is where the image shows it, and `click` closes the dialog. `take_snapshot` is 1920 × 1018 with the status
bar at the bottom and no band at the top. `hover`, unmeasured when this was filed, lights the item it names. A designer drag from
`20,15` to `60,35` moves `Command1` from 40, 40 to 80, 56 on the 8-pixel grid, and `double` opens its code. A drag was a separate
fault of the same kind: the press was platform input and the held moves were raised events, which take their position as the host's,
so it first landed at 64, 16. The same calls with no flag, through XWayland, give the same answers.

An open popup can't be placed under native Wayland, so `take_snapshot` now leaves it out and says why, and a control in one has
no `boundingRect`. That is [#3603](https://github.com/hexide-io/HexIDE/issues/3603).
