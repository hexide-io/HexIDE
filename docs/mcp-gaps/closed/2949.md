# `hover` didn't say how long a tip took to open — **CLOSED** (#2949, 2026-11-08)

**Symptom (2026-11-03, Windows, a Debug build of main at 85d64b98b, headless `claude -p`).** Asked whether a toolbox item shows a tip and after how long, a caller got `no tip opened within 300ms (its ToolTip.ShowDelay may be longer than the watch); declared tip: Picture` with `dwellMs: 300`, and `tip: Picture (its top-left 0 right of and 20 below the point hovered)`, with nothing about timing, with `dwellMs: 600`. Finding the delay took six calls across two rounds.

**Workaround.** Call `hover` with increasing `dwellMs`.

**Fixed (#2949).**
- **A tip that opens:** the reply adds `seen open about 450ms after the move`. The poll looks every 50 ms, so the figure is that close.
- **A declared tip that doesn't open:** the reply quotes the owner's delay, as `its ToolTip.ShowDelay is 400ms`, and adds `longer than the watch: pass a dwellMs above that` when the watch didn't outlast it.
- **Guarded by** `HoverTipTimingTests` and a case in `HoverThroughWindowInputTests` for the control whose delay is read.
