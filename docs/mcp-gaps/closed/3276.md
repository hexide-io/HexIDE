# A form without menus listed an unexplained hidden menu bar — CLOSED (#3276)

**Symptom.** `dump_visual_tree {"interactiveOnly": false}` on a running form with no menus listed an
unnamed, empty, hidden `Window/Menu`. A caller had to guess that it was internal rather than a hidden
control declared by the program. The first-contact measurement is recorded in
[#3276](https://github.com/hexide-io/HexIDE/issues/3276).

**Workaround.** Use `interactiveOnly: true`, which also leaves out other noninteractive controls.

**Fixed.** The control-view walk omits an empty menu bar on a running form under either filter.
Bars with menu items remain subject to the ordinary discovery rules. Headless coverage:
`UiAutomationDriverTests.Runtime_menu_bar_is_listed_only_when_the_form_declares_menus`.
