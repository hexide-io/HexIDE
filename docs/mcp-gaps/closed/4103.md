# `take_snapshot` with `includeFrame` returns a right-to-left window reversed, and doesn't say so — **CLOSED** (#4103, 2026-11-16)

**Symptom.** Measured 2026-11-16 on Windows at 150%, on #3973's first lane round (`9c204b0a6`), whose right-to-left `MsgBox`
has the `WS_EX_LAYOUTRTL` style.

```
take_snapshot {"window":"Title 1","includeFrame":true}
→ the image: "Title 1" at the left with reversed glyphs, the close box at the right, the icon at the left, and the buttons
  Yes, No, Cancel from the left, each label reversed
```

The screen showed the opposite. Its real pixels were grabbed with the window held on top and not activated: the title at the
right and the close box at the left, the icon at the right, and Cancel, No, Yes from the left, with normal glyphs. It's the
capture, not the window. Windows' own `MessageBoxW` with `MB_RTLREADING`, captured with `PrintWindow` and
`PW_RENDERFULLCONTENT`, comes out reversed in the same way. Filed as
[#4103](https://github.com/hexide-io/HexIDE/issues/4103).

**Workaround.** For a window with `WS_EX_LAYOUTRTL` (read `GWL_EXSTYLE`), flip the image horizontally before reading it. To
confirm the layout, grab the screen's pixels with the window held on top.

**Suggested fix.** When the captured window has `WS_EX_LAYOUTRTL`, flip the image before returning it, and say so in the reply.
Failing that, note that the window is mirrored and the image is reversed.

**Fix (#4103).** `NativeWindowFrame.Capture` reads the window's `GWL_EXSTYLE`. When `WS_EX_LAYOUTRTL` is set, it flips each
row of what `PrintWindow` drew, the whole row first and then the crop, so unequal resize borders can't shift it, and the reply
says so: "This window is mirrored (WS_EX_LAYOUTRTL). Windows draws such a window reversed, left for right, when asked for its
pixels, …". The client origin is taken from `GetWindowInfo`'s `rcClient` instead of `ClientToScreen(0, 0)`, which a mirrored
window maps to its client area's right edge, so the reply's offsets would have put every control a client width too far
right. A copy from the screen, the fallback, is already the right way round and is not flipped.

**Live check (hexide-mcp, 2026-11-16, Windows at 150%).** The branch, with #3973's lane commit `9c204b0a6` applied for its
right-to-left box, running `Debug.Print MsgBox("Text of box 2", 35 + 1572864, "Title 2")`:
- `take_snapshot {"window":"Title 2","includeFrame":true}` showed the close box at the left, the icon and "Title 2" at the
  right, and Cancel, No, Yes from the left with normal glyphs, as the screen grab recorded above showed, with the note, and
  `windowOffsetX` 2 (the left border).
- Its client region, cut at that offset, matched `take_snapshot {"window":"Title 2"}`, the compositor's capture, in all 59,052
  pixels. Left reversed, 5,352 would have differed.
- `inspect_element` gave Cancel's bounds as (12, 60, 75.3, 26.7), which the reply's units put at image x 20 to 133 and y 135 to
  175, where the capture shows Cancel.

A popup over a mirrored window was not checked: a message box has none.
