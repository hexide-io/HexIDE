# A click that closes its window reports a missed release — **CLOSED** (#4224, 2026-11-16)

**Symptom:** Double-clicking a floating tool window's title docked the tool and closed its window, but
`interact(target: "Window/Pane[Dock host]/Pane[Root dock]/TitleBar[Tool.immediate]/None[PART_Border]/Text[PART_Title]", action: "click", value: "10,5 double", window: "immediate")`
replied `success: false`, saying nothing took the pointer at the release point. A subsequent
`take_snapshot(window: "immediate")` answered "Unknown window 'immediate'". Retrying the click could
reach whatever was now under the pointer.

**Workaround:** Check `get_tool_windows` or `dump_visual_tree` before retrying that reply.

**Fix:** The click release checks the press's window and actual target before hit-testing. A closed
window or a removed target now produces success, names the first or second press that caused it, and
says nothing was released there. No remaining release or second press is sent. An empty release point
in an open window retains its failure. Dispatcher-driven headless tests cover single-press and
double-tap closure, removal after either press, and the unchanged empty-point failure. The live check
on a floating tool window remains for the supervising session.
